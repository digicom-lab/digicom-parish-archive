The Challenge

Aging Records

Many sacramental books are over 100 years old and deteriorating.

Environmental Risk

Floods, fires, insects, and humidity threaten irreplaceable records.

Manual Retrieval

Parish staff must manually search archives for certificate requests.

The Proposal

A Digital Parish Archive Program designed to preserve sacramental records and improve parish services.

1 Digitize sacramental registers
2 Create searchable parish archive
3 Secure digital preservation
4 Support faster certificate requests

Benefits

For Parishes

  • Faster record retrieval
  • Reduced administrative workload
  • Protection of historical records

For Families

  • Easier certificate requests
  • Support for overseas Filipinos
  • Preservation of family history

For the Church

  • Protection of sacramental heritage
  • Modern administrative tools
  • Preservation of parish archives

Pilot Program

A pilot project with selected parishes to demonstrate the effectiveness of digital parish archives.

  • 5–10 parishes
  • Digitization of sacramental books
  • Deployment of digital archive system
  • Evaluation and diocese expansion
